Intellectual Property & Script Analysis

The Story Engine & Character Universe

Synthesized directly from Jennie Nicassio's 40-page screenplay and 9-page feature beat board.

Primary Character Roster & Voice Specs

Rocky (The Norway Spruce) Lead Protagonist

Small, crooked, and earnest with bent branches and sparse needles. Rocky dreams of standing at Rockefeller Center. His transformation from physical yearning to selfless heroism makes him an unforgettable icon.

"Believe? That's what I'll do! I'll drink lots of water, and I'll grow tall and green!"
VO Profile: Warm, youthful, emotional range (vulnerable to triumphant), ~450 lines.
Mary Louise Guardian Fairy of the Forest

Teacup-sized with iridescent wings, fearless and wise. Refuses to give Rocky magical shortcuts, teaching him that belief requires discipline, resilience, and heart.

"There is no such thing as magic. You just have to believe in yourself without a doubt!"
VO Profile: Sparkling, witty, maternal yet sharp authority, ~280 lines.
Bruce Spruce Antagonist with Redemption Arc

Towering, symmetrical, and arrogant Blue Spruce who bullies Rocky. Breaks under the ice storm and is saved by Rocky, prompting his public confession and respect.

"Between you and me, you don't stand a chance. You're sure to lose!"
VO Profile: Booming baritone, boastful, softening to humble reverence, ~210 lines.
AJ the Squirrel Comic Foil Turned Ally

Hyperactive, sarcastic acrobat who sings taunting rhymes about Rocky before realizing true courage and helping him untie Mary Louise's trap. Stows away to NYC.

"You're limp and bare; you'll never be the Rockefeller Christmas Tree!"
VO Profile: Rapid-fire tempo, comedic singing, emotional sincerity in Act 3, ~320 lines.
Mrs. Pickles (The Skunk) The Vanity Diva

Obsessed with her reflection in a silver hand mirror. When Rocky falls into despair, she places her mirror face-down in the snow in quiet solidarity.

"Nobody look at me until my tail is fluffed! ...Rocky, you have true grandeur."
VO Profile: Theatrical, prim, deadpan humor with hidden warmth, ~110 lines.
Old Oak & Pip the Sapling Chorus & Next Generation

Old Oak holds the memory of past chosen trees. Pip is the tiny sapling who Rocky shields during the ice storm and who carries Rocky's legend into future winters.

"Where does a Rockefeller tree go when the lights go dark? Into the houses of families."
VO Profile: Resonant storybook narration + bright child sapling voice, ~180 lines.

Governance & Creative Alignment

Revisions Policy & Quality Gates

Clear, transparent revision boundaries ensuring creative excellence without schedule slips or hidden fees.

Gate 01
Script & Storyboard Lock
2 rounds of revisions included. Covers panel staging, visual gags, and dialogue flow. Once approved, the script & visual beats are locked.
Gate 02
Visual Assets & Rigs Lock
2 rounds of revisions included. Covers character proportions, color palette bibles, and painted forest backgrounds.
Gate 03
Voice Acting & Timing Lock
2 rounds of pronunciation pickups. Audio timing and dialogue cuts locked before keyframing begins to prevent discarded animation.
Gate 04
Rough Animation Review
2 rounds of revisions included. Covers key poses, timing, lip sync, and emotional nuance before final ink cleanup & VFX.
Gate 05
Final Color & Sound Master
1 final review pass. Minor color grading, lighting glow balances, and sound mix level checks prior to 4K archival master delivery.

Change Orders & Out-of-Scope Alterations

A change order occurs if fundamental changes are requested after a gate has been locked (e.g., rewriting script dialogue after voice recording is finalized, or re-boarding scenes after rough animation is complete). Out-of-scope work is billed transparently at pre-negotiated hourly rates: 2D Animation Rework: $85/hr • Background Repainting: $65/hr • Audio Re-Mix: $75/hr. All change orders require written client authorization via Account Manager Ayaz before execution.
